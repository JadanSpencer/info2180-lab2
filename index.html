My Coding Journey

September 27, 2026 by Jadan Spencer

My coding journey began at 18 years old, fuelled by nothing more than a deepening sense of boredom. It started with a simple Intel computer with 4GB of RAM. It wasn't great, but it got the job done. I was also fortunate to be born in an era where AI tools exist, which helped me work faster and build more projects. That is the beauty I discovered in coding: the ability to create almost anything imaginable in software. With that in mind, I never find myself bored.

My first project was pretty sloppy. It didn't run very well, and I didn't truly understand what I was doing. But I grew through consistent practice, and every project taught me something new, whether it was fixing a stubborn bug or learning a new tool. Now I have built projects that work well and are used in real life.

Football and Focus

October 3, 2026 by Jadan Spencer

Coding for hours can drain your energy, and that is why I play football. An evening on the pitch clears my head and lets me return to my screen with fresh ideas. Problems that looked impossible before the game often become simple afterwards, because I am no longer staring at the same lines of code.

Football has also taught me lessons that apply to development. Good teams communicate, trust each other and practise the basics until they become second nature. Those are the same habits that help when working on a group project, reviewing code or learning a new language. Balancing both keeps me sharp, and it keeps studying fun.
